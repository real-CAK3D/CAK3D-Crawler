Privacy policy

CAK3D Crawler · Last updated October 10, 2026

CAK3D Crawler is made by CAK3D. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the CAK3D Crawler app for Android and the CAK3D Crawler website. The short version: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and what you crawl stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • The texts, PDFs, books and photos you open, and everything the app makes from them: keywords, topics, egg sacs, notes, flashcards and study progress.
  • Your library of saved crawls and your settings, including what the app learns about your interests.
  • Your AI settings, including any API key you enter. The key is only sent to the AI service you chose.
  • Reading text out loud uses your device's built-in voices.
  • Reading the text in scanned pages and photos (OCR) happens on your device. Images are not uploaded.

This information is kept in your device's app storage. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, deletes it. We never receive a copy.

When the app connects to other services

Some features need the internet. They only run when you use them, and they send only what that feature needs:

  • Opening a web page or crawling a site: the address you enter is fetched from Wikipedia (for Wikipedia pages) or through the Jina reader service (r.jina.ai) for other sites.
  • Searching the web: your search words are sent to DuckDuckGo through the Jina reader service, or to Wikipedia's search.
  • Summaries and answers from an AI: the passages being summarized and your question are sent to the AI service you set up in Settings (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq, or a model on your own computer), under that service's own privacy policy. If you don't set one up, nothing is sent.
  • Fonts and reading tools: the app may download fonts from Google Fonts, and the language data used for OCR from jsDelivr, the first time they're needed.

These services receive your device's internet address, as with any web request, and handle it under their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Paid packs are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and tells the app which packs your Google account owns. We don't see or store your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • We don't sell or share your information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
