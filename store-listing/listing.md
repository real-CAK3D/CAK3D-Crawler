# Google Play store listing: Arachnotes

## App name (10 / 30)
Arachnotes

## Short description (80 max)
A spider reads any text and spins it into topics, study notes and citations.

## Full description (4000 max)
Arachnotes turns anything you read into notes you can study and cite. Give it a web page, a PDF, a book or a photo of a page, and a spider crawls it keyword by keyword, sorts every keyword into a topic, and ties each one back to the sentence it came from.

OPEN ALMOST ANYTHING
• Paste text, open a link, or search the web and crawl several pages together
• Share a link, text, file or photo to Arachnotes from any app and it starts crawling
• Wikipedia articles come in clean, with their pictures, captions and licenses
• PDF (with page numbers for citations), EPUB, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OpenDocument, RTF, saved web pages, Markdown, subtitles, CSV and JSON
• Scanned pages and photos are read on your device with text recognition

KEYWORDS AND TOPICS
• The spider reads each keyword in context and decides which topic it belongs to
• Live stats show every decision and how sure the spider is; the unsure ones come to you
• Type a focus topic and the spider hunts the matching keywords first
• Tap any keyword to see its topic, the sentence it came from and the whole paragraph
• From the web: a short summary from Wikipedia or a dictionary for each keyword when you're online, with Read next suggestions for every topic (or turn it off)

SEE IT AS A WEB
• Web: the subject at the hub and a topic on every spoke; tap a topic to open its own web
• Nest: drag and zoom every keyword on a map that remembers your layout
• Graph and Timeline: charts of the topics, and where each one turns up in the text
• Media: the pictures and videos from the pages you crawled, plus Wikipedia pictures about your keywords, sorted by topic and cited

STUDY WITH IT
• A study guide with key points, terms and questions, saved as Markdown or a web page
• Flashcards made from your keywords, with a fact from the web on the back, scheduled with spaced repetition
• Egg sacs: finished topics wrapped up with a summary and their sources
• Notes on any keyword or passage, and any passage read aloud

CITE AS YOU GO
• Every keyword, note and answer points back to its sentence, numbered by source and page
• APA, MLA or Chicago style, a bibliography, and export to Zotero and BibTeX

ASK THE TEXT
• Ask a question and get the passages that answer it, with citations
• A free AI that runs on your device, downloaded once and working offline, for short written answers and summaries
• Or connect your own AI (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini and others) with your own API key. Your key stays on your device

READ CODE
• Open code files, a .zip of a project or a GitHub link
• Every function, class and variable mapped as a web, with where each is defined, used and called, and the whole codebase explained in plain words

MAKE IT YOURS
• Choose the spider, its look, how it hunts and the webs it spins, plus themes, weather and sounds
• Start on the guide, where you left off, or a blank page
• A short walkthrough the first time, and the guide shows every feature with moving examples
• Leave mid-crawl and the spider keeps going; a notification tells you when it's done
• Sounds made on your device: tiny crawling footsteps, rain, crickets, wind or a crackling fire, and music
• A battery saver that rests the spider while you're away

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No accounts, no ads and no tracking. Error reports are only sent if you say yes
• Your texts, notes and library stay on your device and work offline once opened
• Save crawls to your library, move them between devices with a weave code or crawl file, and back everything up

Every reading and study feature is free. Extra spider kinds, looks, hats, themes, weather and presets are optional in-app purchases, singly or in bundles, and a bundle includes anything added to it later.
