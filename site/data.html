Delete your data

Arachnotes by CAK3D_Creations · Last updated October 15, 2026

Arachnotes has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. This page explains what we hold about you and how to delete it. The only data we ever receive is the issue and error reports you choose to send, any fixes we leave for your app, and anything you post in the public Issues and Ideas forum.

What can be deleted, and how

  • Issue reports, error reports and fixes: deleted straight away when you send the form below with your support ID.
  • Link codes and linked browsers: deleted straight away too. Browsers you linked to the app go back to the free web version.
  • Transfer codes: nothing to ask for. The locked data is deleted when it's picked up, or after 24 hours.
  • Forum posts and replies: removed within 30 days of your request. Include the links to them in the form.
  • Everything on your device (your texts, notes, library, settings and AI keys): this never reaches us. Delete it yourself by uninstalling the app, or with Android Settings → Apps → Arachnotes → Storage → Clear storage. In the browser version, clear the website's data in your browser.

Step by step

  • 1. In the app, open Settings, then the ? (Help & support).
  • 2. Find Your support ID, which looks like AN-7K3Q-9XWD-2M4P, and tap Copy.
  • 3. Paste it into the form below. If you also want forum posts removed, paste their links.
  • 4. Press Delete my data. Reports, error reports and fixes for that ID are deleted at once, and the page tells you how many.

If you've uninstalled the app and no longer have your support ID, list your forum post links, or use the form to tell us the email address you gave in a report, and we'll find and delete it.

What is kept

We keep a short record of each request (the support ID, the post links you listed, and when it was done) for 12 months, so we can show the deletion was carried out. It is then deleted automatically. Reports we haven't been asked to delete are deleted after 12 months anyway.
